Robots that fix themselves

Someday idea! Columbia scientists made "Truss Links": robot sticks with magnets on the ends, like Geomag toys. They snap together into shapes, crawl, and swap out a dead stick for a good one they find.

🧲 Try now: build triangles and diamonds with magnet sticks or straws, then "fix" one by swapping a piece!

Source: Robot Metabolism

DIY Drone (someday)

A tiny X-frame quadcopter with 4 propellers and a mini breadboard on top holding a small blue sensor board (looks like a motion/gyro sensor). Seen in a reel, looks like it's from Schematik.

✈️❌ A real flying drone needs special parts that aren't in our kit: fast brushless motors, motor controllers, a flight battery, and a gyro sensor. #12 is too weak to lift off.

✅ Now: spin a propeller with P13 Mini Fan and feel the wind! 🌬️

Source tool: Schematik

Drone with jet wheels (someday)

Someday idea! A drone builder (QuadMover) swapped the flat propellers for 3D-printed spinning wheels with fins inside. It still flew, and it sounds like a little jet instead of a buzz!

Learn ESP32 in 3D

A free website with 3D lessons you can spin around. It shows exactly where each wire and part goes, plus a mini wiring map. Lesson 2 flashes two LEDs!

🧠 The ESP32 is a little board like our #1 Uno, but it also has Wi-Fi and Bluetooth built in. It's not in our kit.

✅ Now: open the site together and spin the 3D board. Then light two LEDs from #38 on #29 like the picture shows.

Gears that make power

A crank turns big gears, the big gears spin a small gear really fast, and the fast spin turns a motor backwards into a generator. That makes electricity, like a hand-crank flashlight!

🔄 Big gear to small gear = slow turn becomes fast spin. Fast spin = more power.

✅ Now: touch an LED from #38 to the two wires of #12 (no battery!). Wrap string around the #12 shaft and pull fast. Does the LED flicker? Flip the LED if not.

Source: Voxel Penguin reel

RoverKit: build a robot car in 3D

A free website where you build a robot car step by step: gather the parts, put on 4 wheels and motors, wire the driver board, then drive it in a simulator.

🔌 It uses an ESP32 board, but the ideas match your kit: motors like #12, a motor driver like #25 L293D, wheels, and wires.

✅ Now: open it together and play "spot the part". Which pieces look like ones in your kit?
